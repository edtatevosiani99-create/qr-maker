QR Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QR Studio handles information when you use the app.

Information handled by the app

QR Studio is designed to scan and create QR codes. Information contained in a QR code that you choose to scan or create may be processed to provide the app's features. Do not scan or share QR codes containing information you do not want to disclose.

The developer does not intentionally collect personal information through the app or operate a server to store QR code contents. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated.

Permissions

The app may request camera access to scan QR codes. Camera access is used for this feature. You can manage app permissions in your device settings.

Third-party services

The app may be distributed through Google Play. If advertising or analytics services are included in the version you use, their providers may process device or usage information under their own privacy policies. This page should be updated to name those providers if such services are added.

Data sharing and retention

The developer does not sell personal information. QR code content that you choose to save or share remains under your control, including through your device's features and settings.

Children's privacy

QR Studio is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Please use care when scanning, saving, or sharing sensitive information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
